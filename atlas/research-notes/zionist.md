## Source quality (read this)
Most biographical anchors come from English Wikipedia articles fetched 2026-10-10, plus Jewish Virtual Library congress summaries, the Ben-Gurion House timeline, FRUS (Council of Ten minutes, 27 Feb 1919) and a HathiTrust catalog record (Jabotinsky evidence, 11 Feb 1937). These are tier-2 sources. Several sources (Jewish Encyclopedia, COJS, Center for Israel Education, JWA caption, Sheffer 1996, JRB, HathiTrust, Wikipedia Mikveh Israel) were seen only as search-result excerpts; their source notes say so and they have no URL. Primary/scholarly verification still needed: Central Zionist Archives, Ben-Gurion Archives, Jabotinsky Institute, Segev, Shapira, Shlaim, Khalidi etc. No Palestinian-authored source was used for this cluster; where Palestinian perspectives matter (Nebi Musa, Peel, partition) existing sources khalidi-2006 / mattar-2000 are reused.

## Could not verify / uncertain
- Ben-Gurion arrival at Jaffa: 7 Sep 1906 (Wikipedia) vs 9 Sep (Ben-Gurion House); month precision used. Early dates (Sejera, Salonica, Istanbul) are year-level.
- Herzl at Mikveh Israel: 28 vs 29 Oct 1898; Pius X audience 25 vs 26 Jan 1904; Third Congress 15-17/18/19 Aug 1899.
- Congress dates differ by source for 14th, 17th, 19th, 20th (month precision used).
- Jabotinsky arrest/sentencing dates (April 1920) and release (about July 1920) not found; Acre observation is month-level and approximate. His Alexandria (Zion Mule Corps) and Paris 1925 presence not verified.
- Ben-Yehuda imprisonment: Dec 1893 (Wikipedia) vs 1894 elsewhere; birthplace Luzhki not mapped.
- Ahad Ha'am birth day (18 Aug) depends on calendar conversion. Ben-Zvi and Meir death dates are from general knowledge; the fetched excerpts did not state them.
- Ben-Zvi signing the Declaration in person on 14 May 1948 is unresolved (associated only). Ussishkin at the 1897 Congress and Ahad Ha'am at the First Congress could not be confirmed.
- Ruppin and Weizmann at the Hague 1907 only associated (Ruppin's attendance not stated).
- Elyashar and Uziel records are thin (single short article each).
