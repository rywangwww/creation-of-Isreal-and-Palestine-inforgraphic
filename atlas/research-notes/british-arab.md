## Method and caveats
- Dates were cross-checked by fetching English Wikipedia articles (used only as `reference` sources, ids `wp-*`) plus a few web searches; underlying scholarship (Wilson, Fromkin, Kedourie, Storrs, Shlaim, etc.) is cited by real bibliographic entry but the books themselves were NOT consulted page-by-page. All records are `draft`.
- `odnb` is cited generically for birth/death dates of several people; individual ODNB entries were not fetched. Birth/death dates for Allenby, Samuel, Churchill, Lloyd George, Curzon, Bevin, Cunningham, Storrs (death), Wauchope (birth), Bell etc. come from fetched Wikipedia or general knowledge and should be checked against ODNB.
- Wherever I could not confirm a day, I used month/year precision and `approximate`.

## Not verified / problems found
- Wikipedia's Lawrence article gives "February 1920" for his Colonial Office role; Churchill became Colonial Secretary in Feb 1921, so 1921 is used. Check Wilson 1989.
- Wejh: Lawrence was NOT at the fighting (23-24 Jan 1917); he arrived about two days later. The place is tracked as presence only.
- Hussein bin Ali: birth year 1853/54; death date 4 June vs 4 July 1931 conflict; his Cyprus town is unconfirmed (Nicosia used).
- Cairo Conference: attendance lists (incl. Allenby, Ja'far al-Askari) come from a single secondary list; the Cairo-to-Jerusalem sequence and exact departure dates were not found. Obs cover only 12 March.
- Churchill-Abdullah meeting date (28 March 1921) and attendees rest on Abdullah's memoirs as summarised by a web page located by search (cojs.org) and Wikipedia/Gilbert summaries; the memoir itself was not read.
- Picot's birth place/date and death place were not verified (birth obs on Paris omitted; no death obs). Sykes-Picot "initialled 3 Jan 1916" place (London) is inferred. Also: Wikipedia's Sykes article says he visited Palestine in March 1916 to meet Weizmann, which looks wrong (Weizmann met him in London in Feb 1917) and was ignored.
- MacMichael ambush location not found (placed at region level).
- Wingate: Night Squads end date, and Addis Ababa presence in May 1941, are approximate.
- Jericho Conference (1 Dec 1948) date from memory/Shlaim; not fetched.
- Bevin 18 Feb 1947 statement date from memory; Hansard cited without column numbers.
- Hebrew University 1925 opening: Wikipedia also lists Churchill as present; no second source, so omitted.
- Faisal-Weizmann Aqaba meeting (4 June 1918): Lawrence's presence is disputed in the literature; left unpatched. Council of Ten 6 Feb 1919: Lawrence/Nuri al-Said as delegation members is reported by a web summary only; not patched.
